# Known issues

## Pre-race car selection and purchase

After the car-selection animation settles, A can change the car class instead of activating the visible Continue or Buy action.

### Workaround

Press B to leave the screen, A to re-enter, then A again immediately, before the animation settles. The later modifications screen works normally.

## Mouse cursor

Press **Select** to activate or deactivate the virtual mouse cursor. While the cursor is active, use the stick or D-pad to move it and A/B to interact with menus. Start keeps its normal pause/map function.

## Reporting

When reporting a problem, include:

- TrimUI Smart Pro S and SpruceOS version;
- package version and supported game version;
- exact reproduction steps;
- the relevant part of `Roms/PORTS/nfsmw/logs/nfsmw.log`.

Do not upload game files, extracted libraries, assets, audio, or saves.
